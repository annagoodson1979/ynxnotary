Documents

Documents I Sign

  • Public-service notarizations and sworn statements
  • Legal and estate-planning documents
  • Loan-signing packages and real-estate documents
  • Business and corporate agreements
  • Healthcare-related authorizations and directives
  • IPEN appointments for eligible in-person electronic notarization

Documents I Don't Sign

  • Vital records such as birth, death, and marriage certificates
  • Court-filed records and judgments
  • Official government-issued records that must come from the issuing agency

If you are unsure, reach out first and I can help point you to the correct next step.

Quick Definitions

  • Acknowledgment: you confirm the signature is yours and that you signed willingly.
  • Jurat: you sign in front of the notary and swear the contents are true.
  • Copy Certification by Document Custodian: the notary notarizes your sworn statement that the copy is true and correct.
